## Key Decisions Made
* **Retention of `jsconfig.json`**: Confirmed that `frontend/jsconfig.json` is **required** and must remain in the repository. A codebase search revealed 25 distinct occurrences across 17 frontend files leveraging the `@/*` path alias mapping to `./src/*`. Removing it would immediately disrupt IDE path resolution and compilation pipelines.
* **State-Driven Workflow Over Hard Routing**: For maximum local stability and speed within the current workspace iteration, the detailed views (Intake Submission Review panel and Confirmed Booking detail cards) were modeled inside `BookingListPage.jsx` using modular local components rather than multi-file reactive router links.
